# SAP HCM Time Management Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the fundamental concepts and processes of
SAP HCM Time Management.

## Objectives

- Understand SAP HCM Time Management
- Maintain employee work schedules
- Understand attendance and absence management
- Understand leave management
- Maintain employee time-related information
- Understand integration between Time Management and Payroll

## Modules / Topics Covered

- Work Schedules
- Attendance Management
- Absence Management
- Leave Management
- Time Data
- Time Evaluation – Basic Concepts
- Integration with Payroll

## Project Outcome

Gained practical knowledge of SAP HCM Time Management processes and
understood how employee time data is maintained and used in HR and
payroll processes.
